Group of Measures Used to Illustrate (KPIs) : 

Total Employees = COUNTROWS(HR_Employees)

Attrition Count = CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(HR_Employees), HR_Employees[Attrition] = "Yes")

Active Employees = CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(HR_Employees), HR_Employees[Attrition] = "No")

Attrition Rate = DIVIDE([Attrition Count], [Total Employees], 0)

Avg Age = AVERAGE(HR_Employees[Age])

Avg Monthly Income = AVERAGE(HR_JobDetails[MonthlyIncome])

Avg Job Satisfaction = AVERAGE(HR_Satisfaction[JobSatisfaction])

Avg Years At Company = AVERAGE(HR_WorkHistory[YearsAtCompany])

Avg Num Companies Worked = AVERAGE(HR_WorkHistory[NumCompaniesWorked])

Employees At Risk After Raise = ROUND([Total Employees] * [Projected Attrition Reduction], 0)


------------------------

Group of Measures Used to Answer Prediction Questions : 

Attrition Risk Score = 
VAR OverTimeScore     = IF(MAX(HR_JobDetails[OverTime]) = "Yes", 30, 0)
VAR SatisfactionScore = IF(MAX(HR_Satisfaction[JobSatisfaction]) <= 2, 25, 0)
VAR BalanceScore      = IF(MAX(HR_Satisfaction[WorkLifeBalance]) <= 2, 20, 0)
VAR DistanceScore     = IF(MAX(HR_Employees[DistanceFromHome]) >= 20, 15, 0)
VAR StockScore        = IF(MAX(HR_JobDetails[StockOptionLevel]) = 0, 10, 0)
RETURN
OverTimeScore + SatisfactionScore + BalanceScore + DistanceScore + StockScore

Risk Category = 
IF([Attrition Risk Score] >= 51, "🔴 High Risk",
IF([Attrition Risk Score] >= 21, "🟡 Medium Risk", "🟢 Low Risk"))

------------------------

Measures Used in the Parameters:

Projected Income =
[Avg Monthly Income] * (1 + 'Salary Increase %'[Salary Increase % Value] / 100)

Projected Attrition Reduction =
[Attrition Rate] * (1 - 'Salary Increase %'[Salary Increase % Value] / 200)

Projected Income After Experience =
[Avg Monthly Income] *
(1 + ('Extra Years'[Extra Years Value] * 0.05))

-----------------------

Parameters : 

Extra Years = GENERATESERIES(0, 10, 1)
Extra Years Value = SELECTEDVALUE('Extra Years'[Extra Years], 5)


Salary Increase % = GENERATESERIES(0, 50, 1)
Salary Increase % Value = SELECTEDVALUE('Salary Increase %'[Salary Increase %])
